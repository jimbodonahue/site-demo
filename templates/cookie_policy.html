Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies. We are committed to minimizing the use of cookies and tracking technologies to ensure your privacy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser's directory by websites you visit. They are typically used to maintain user sessions, remember preferences, and track analytics.

2. Essential Cookies We Use

We do not use marketing, profiling, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. We only use strictly necessary cookies for core site security and forum identity:

  • Django Session Cookie (sessionid): This cookie is set when you access the forum or restore an identity passkey. It maintains your temporary session state and anonymous identity. It does not contain any personal information.
  • CSRF Token Cookie (csrftoken): This cookie protects you and our site against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks on interactive forms. It is strictly necessary for security purposes.

3. Optional Anonymous Usage Metrics

If you choose Accept in the consent banner, we enable first-party anonymous usage metrics. These are not third-party analytics cookies. Instead, your browser stores a random anonymous ID in localStorage and periodically sends usage events (page time, code runs, resets, evaluations, and scenario selections) to our own servers.

We use this information only to improve the platform. Metrics do not include notebook source code, names, emails, or advertising profiles.

4. Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to store exercise progress, theme preferences, consent choice, and (if accepted) the anonymous metrics ID. Progress and theme data stay on your device unless you explicitly export a Passkey Bundle.

5. Declining Cookies

If you choose Reject in the cookie consent banner, optional anonymous usage metrics are disabled and any stored anonymous metrics ID is removed. Session cookies needed for security may still apply when you use the forum. You can still use the platform's exercises and coding spaces.
