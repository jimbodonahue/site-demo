Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies. We minimize tracking to protect your privacy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by websites you visit. They are typically used to maintain sessions, remember preferences, and (on other sites) support analytics.

2. Essential Cookies We Use

We do not use marketing, profiling, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. We only use strictly necessary cookies for core site security and membership sessions:

  • Django Session Cookie (sessionid): Set when you use My Account, the forum, or restore a passkey. It maintains your temporary session and passkey identity. It does not contain your passkey string.
  • CSRF Token Cookie (csrftoken): Protects interactive forms against Cross-Site Request Forgery. Strictly necessary for security.

3. Optional Anonymous Usage Metrics

If you choose Accept in the consent banner, we enable first-party anonymous usage metrics. These are not third-party analytics cookies. Your browser stores a random anonymous ID in localStorage and periodically sends usage events (page time, code runs, resets, evaluations, and scenario selections) to our servers.

We use this only to improve the platform. Metrics do not include notebook source code, names, emails, or advertising profiles.

4. Local Storage

We may use your browser's localStorage for theme preference, cookie consent choice, and (if accepted) the anonymous metrics ID. Exercise progress and attempt history are stored on our servers with your passkey membership—not as the primary progress store in localStorage.

5. Declining Cookies

If you choose Reject in the consent banner, optional anonymous usage metrics are disabled and any stored anonymous metrics ID is removed. Session cookies needed for security may still apply when you use the forum or My Account. You can still practice exercises.
