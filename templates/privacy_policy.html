Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

We prioritize your privacy above all else. This platform is designed to be privacy-first, collecting no personal identifiers (such as emails, names, or passwords) for public users.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or store any emails, passwords, names, or IP logs that link back to your real-world identity. You do not need to register with an email to use the platform's exercises or discussion forum.

2. Local Storage of Exercise Progress

Your interactive notebook coding progress and attempt histories are stored primarily on your own machine in your browser's local storage (localStorage). Notebook source code is not used for marketing or profiling.

3. Anonymous Forum Identity & Passkeys

When you participate in the community discussion forums, we generate an anonymous token-based user profile on the server. This profile is identified solely by a 32-character hexadecimal token (Passkey) which you can download as a JSON bundle file to your own machine. If you lose this passkey and clear your browser session, we cannot recover your forum posts or exercise progress because we hold no email association.

4. Anonymous Usage Metrics

If you accept cookies/preferences in the consent banner, we collect first-party anonymous usage metrics to understand how the learning platform is used and to improve exercises. These metrics may include:

  • Which pages or exercises were viewed
  • Approximate time spent on a page (active time while the tab is visible)
  • How often code is run, reset, or evaluated
  • Which difficulty or scenario options were selected

Events are tied only to a random anonymous ID created in your browser. We do not store names, emails, IP addresses, or notebook source code in these metrics. Rejecting consent disables this collection. You can clear the anonymous ID by rejecting consent or clearing site data in your browser.

5. Cookies

We use strictly necessary session cookies solely to authenticate your requests on the forum and handle identity restoration. No third-party tracking, profiling, or advertising cookies are utilized. Read our Cookie Policy for details.
