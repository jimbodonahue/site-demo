Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

We prioritize your privacy. This platform is designed to be privacy-first: public learners do not need an email or password to practice or join the forum.

1. Information We Do Not Collect From Learners

For regular membership we do not require emails, passwords, or legal names. Staff and administrator accounts are separate and may use email plus password to manage the site.

2. Account, Exercise Progress & Passkeys

When you use My Account or the forum, we create a token-based membership on our servers. Your identity is a hexadecimal passkey you can download. Exercise notebook progress, attempt history, badges, and forum activity are stored on the server and linked to that passkey—not primarily in your browser.

If you lose your passkey and clear your session, we cannot recover your account because we hold no learner email for reset. Optional one-time passkey emails (if you request them) are not stored as an account email.

3. Profile Details You Choose To Share

If you save a nickname, avatar, short intro, or About Me text, that information is stored with your membership and may appear on your public profile. You can request account deletion from My Account; data is removed within 48 hours.

4. Anonymous Usage Metrics

If you accept cookies/preferences in the consent banner, we collect first-party anonymous usage metrics to understand how the learning platform is used and to improve exercises. These metrics may include:

  • Which pages or exercises were viewed
  • Approximate time spent on a page (active time while the tab is visible)
  • How often code is run, reset, or evaluated
  • Which difficulty or scenario options were selected

Events are tied only to a random anonymous ID created in your browser. We do not store names, emails, IP addresses, or notebook source code in these metrics. Rejecting consent disables this collection.

5. Cookies

We use strictly necessary session cookies to keep you signed in to your passkey session and to protect forms. No third-party tracking, profiling, or advertising cookies are used. Read our Cookie Policy for details.
